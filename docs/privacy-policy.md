# Privacy Policy for 36 Pics

**Effective date:** 21 November 2026
**App:** 36 Pics (Android and iOS, app ID `com.ankit.thirtysixpics`)
**Developer:** Ankit Anurag
**Contact:** privacy@toormai.com

## The short version

36 Pics does not collect your data. Your photos and rolls stay on your device. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see, receive or store anything you create in the app.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is never sent anywhere:

- **Photos** you take with the in-app camera. Each photo is processed on your device to apply the film look you chose (Classic, Noir or Faded '70s).
- **Roll details**: the name you give each roll, which film stock each photo used, and the times a roll was started, finished and set to develop.
- **Your settings**: your chosen theme and how long a roll takes to develop.

Photos taken in the app are private to the app. They do not appear in your Photos or Gallery unless you choose to save them (see below).

## Permissions the app asks for, and why

| Permission | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | To shoot photos with the in-app viewfinder. | When you first open the camera. |
| **Photos / storage (add only)** | To save a developed roll to your Photos library or Gallery, only when you tap "Save to Photos". | When you first save. On Android 10 and newer no storage permission is needed. |

The app does **not** request or use your location, microphone, contacts, calendar, files, or any other personal data. It does not record audio or video.

## Saving to your Photos library

When you tap **Save to Photos**, the app copies the photos from that roll into your device's Photos or Gallery, either as a new album named after the roll or as plain photos. This happens entirely on your device. After they are saved, those copies belong to your Photos or Gallery and are managed by your device's own settings and any cloud backup you have enabled for it (for example iCloud Photos or Google Photos). That backup is between you and that service, not 36 Pics.

## Sharing and third parties

We do not share your data with anyone, because we do not have it. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not connect to the internet.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- To delete a roll and its photos, press and hold it in the Rolls tab and confirm.
- Deleting the app removes all roll data and settings stored by it.
- Copies you saved to your Photos or Gallery are not removed by deleting a roll or the app. Delete those from your Photos or Gallery app.

## Children

36 Pics is a general-audience camera app and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

## Security

Your data is held in the app's private storage, which the operating system protects from other apps. Because nothing leaves your device, there is no server-side copy that could be exposed. Your device's own protections (screen lock, device encryption, backups) apply to the app's data.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on our side. If you have a question about this policy, contact us at the address above.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding an account or cloud sync, we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in the app's release notes.
