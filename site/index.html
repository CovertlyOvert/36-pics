One roll. 36 shots.
Then you wait.

36 Pics is a camera that brings back the discipline of a disposable film camera. Load a roll, shoot up to 36 exposures, and let it develop before you see a single photo.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Load, shoot, develop, relive

Name a roll and pick a film stock. Shoot with a live viewfinder, then wait. You choose how long a roll takes to develop, from 15 seconds to a week, and the countdown keeps running while the app is closed.

Three film stocks

Classic is warm and contrasty. Noir is black and white with deep shadows. Faded ’70s is low contrast with a warm cast. The look is baked into each photo as you shoot, and you can switch stock mid-roll.

Two themes

Vintage is cream and brown, like a photo album. Darkroom is near-black with a safelight red.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet connection. Your photos stay on your device until you choose to save them to your library. Read the privacy policy.
