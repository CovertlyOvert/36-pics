Support

Questions, bugs or feedback about 36 Pics? Email support@toormai.com. Please include your phone model and OS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong.

Common questions

Where are my photos?

Photos you take are stored privately inside the app, on your device only. They do not appear in your Photos or Gallery until you open a developed roll and tap Save to Photos.

Why can’t I see my roll yet?

A finished roll has to develop first, like film. You choose the wait on the You screen (the person icon on Home and Rolls), from 15 seconds to 1 week. A new setting applies to rolls you finish from then on. Want to see a roll sooner? Use Abandon film on Home to end a roll early; it develops in 5 seconds.

Can I change the film stock?

Yes. Pick a starting stock when you load a roll, and switch between Classic, Noir and Faded ’70s from the camera at any time. A roll shot on more than one stock is labelled Mixed.

How do I delete a roll?

Press and hold the roll in the Rolls tab and confirm. This removes the roll and its photos from the app. Copies you already saved to Photos or Gallery stay there until you delete them yourself.

Can I get a deleted roll or photo back?

No. Everything lives only on your device, so there is no copy for us to restore. If you want to keep a roll, save it to Photos first.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. 36 Pics works fully offline and has no accounts.

What happens to my rolls if I delete the app?

They are removed with it. Save anything you want to keep to Photos or Gallery first.
